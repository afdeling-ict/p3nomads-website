Privacy

We keep this short, because we collect very little. This notice explains what happens to your personal data when you visit this website or book a meeting with us.

Who we are

P3nomads B.V., based in the Netherlands and registered with the Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 96973765, is responsible for the processing of personal data described in this notice.

For any question about your personal data, write to farid@p3nomads.com.

Visiting this website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no forms. Fonts, images and code are loaded from this website itself, not from other companies.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When a GitHub Pages site is visited, GitHub logs the visitor's IP address for security purposes. We have no access to these logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Booking a meeting

The meeting buttons take you to our booking page at Cal.com. There you enter your name, your email address and, if you wish, a short message. We use this to arrange and prepare the meeting.

Cal.com processes this data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. Cal.com, Inc. is based in the United States; it states that transfers of personal data from the European Union are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

For data that Cal.com processes for its own purposes, its own privacy policy applies.

Emailing us

If you email us, we use your message and contact details to answer you and to follow up on your question.

Why we may use your data

We process booking and contact data to take steps you ask for before a possible assignment, and because we have a legitimate interest in answering your questions and preparing our conversations.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and contact data no longer than necessary, and at most two years after our last contact. If our contact leads to an assignment, Dutch law requires us to keep business records for seven years.

Your rights

You may ask us to show, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or stop its use, or to send it to you in a usable format. Email farid@p3nomads.com and we will respond within one month.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

We update this notice when our website or the way we work changes. Last updated: 27 November 2026.

Questions about this notice?

We are happy to explain how we handle your data. Book a short call, or write to us at the address above.

A 15-minute call, booked directly in Farid's calendar. Wherever you are, you are welcome.
