Competitive dialogue

When a government knows its challenge but not yet the best solution, it can shape that solution together with the market. We guide those tenders, and prepare the teams that lead them.

Talk about your tender See the simulation

Tendering as a conversation.

In a competitive dialogue, the contracting authority talks with a small group of selected candidates in rounds, before they submit their final tenders. Solutions improve along the way, and the authority learns what the market can really offer.

Three things to get right.

A dialogue only works when every candidate trusts the process. That trust rests on three principles, and they are where most teams struggle.

  • Transparency

    Candidates know in advance how the dialogue runs, what is discussed and how the final tenders will be judged.

  • Equal treatment

    Every candidate gets the same information and the same opportunities, in every round.

  • Confidentiality

    A candidate's ideas stay its own. What is said at one table is never passed to another.

The dialogue simulation: a whole tender in one day.

The best preparation for a dialogue is to have done one. In our simulation your team goes through every phase, from the theory and the do's and don'ts to holding the conversations themselves.

The case
Dryland, a fictional municipality that wants to become a smart city through a public-private partnership. It is based on a real, completed tender by the municipality of Hilversum.
The roles
Each participant plays either the municipality or one of the consortia in the dialogue, so everyone feels both sides of the table.
The format
One day, on location or online, with six to eight participants per simulation.
