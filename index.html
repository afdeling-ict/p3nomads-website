One table, many partners.

No road, port or coastline is built by one party alone. We bring together everyone your project needs, and keep them together.

  • PublicPublic

    Authorities that share one project, but not one budget or mandate.

    For example: Rijnkade Arnhem, where a water board and a city build one riverfront.

  • PublicPrivate

    A government and the private partner that will design, build, finance or operate.

    For example: Naivasha and Sabaki, two water PPPs in preparation in Kenya.

  • PrivatePrivate

    Consultants, contractors and investors who are stronger as one consortium.

    For example: Water as Leverage Cartagena, a consortium of eight partners.

A good partnership also has to pay its way.

Financiers commit when the project itself earns enough to carry it. So we design the partnership, the money and the revenues together.

Who is at the table, and in what role?

A clear division of roles and risks between the partners is the foundation everything else rests on.

Who pays in the end, and who pays first?

Funding ultimately pays for the project. Financing is advanced and repaid. We help find the right mix, and the right moment to approach donors, banks and investors.

What does the project earn once it runs?

Tariffs, availability payments, concessions, compatible economic activity. We look for income that fits the project and its surroundings.

Would a lender say yes?

We test revenues, risks and contracts against a financier's standard early, while the structure is still easy to change.

From signed contract to working asset.

We stay involved through procurement and contract management, so the partnership keeps working after the ceremony.
