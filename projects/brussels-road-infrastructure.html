Brussels, Belgium

Spending review of Brussels road infrastructure

Getting more out of every euro the region spends on its roads, bridges and tunnels.

Location
Brussels-Capital Region, Belgium
Scope
331 km of regional roads, 92 bridges and flyovers, 30 tunnels
Period
2021
Our role
Spending review: procurement and contracting

The project

The Brussels region manages 331 km of regional roads, 92 bridges and flyovers and 30 tunnels, and spends around 199 million euros a year on maintenance and new projects. An ambitious investment plan for the tunnels and a backlog in road maintenance put pressure on the budget.

The challenge

Institutional complexity and heavy administrative procedures make infrastructure hard to manage in Brussels. The review had to find practical ways to make road policy and its delivery more efficient and effective.

What we did

  • Analysed how maintenance and new projects are procured, and which policy instruments are used.
  • Explored alternatives, including integrated contract forms, framework agreements and benchmarking against other infrastructure managers.
  • Interviewed managers about their experience with outsourcing.
  • Set out actionable policy options in the review report.
