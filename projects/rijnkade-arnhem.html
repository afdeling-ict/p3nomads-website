Arnhem, the Netherlands

Rijnkade Arnhem

A water board and a city building one riverfront together, under one agreement.

Location
Rijnkade, Arnhem
Parties
Waterschap Rijn en IJssel and Gemeente Arnhem
Period
2022
Our role
Realisation agreement

The project

Along the Rijnkade in Arnhem, the water board and the municipality carry out their works as one integrated project, tendered together.

The challenge

Two public bodies, each with its own tasks, budget and risks, had to agree how to share one project: who pays for what, who carries which risk, and how they work together.

What we did

  • Drafted the realisation agreement between the water board and the municipality.
  • Divided the scope along the object tree, and shared costs that cross the scope with an agreed allocation key.
  • Allocated risks along the same lines.
  • Set up the joint project organisation and the way the parties decide together.
