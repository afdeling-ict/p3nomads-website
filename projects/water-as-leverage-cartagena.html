Cartagena, Colombia

Water as Leverage Cartagena

Nature-based plans to make Cartagena's lagoon and canals safer and cleaner, and the search for the money to build them.

Location
Ciénaga de la Virgen and Canal Juan Angola, Cartagena
In cooperation with
Alcaldía de Cartagena
Funded by
The Dutch government
Led by
Arcadis
Period
2022 to 2024

The project

Cartagena struggles with flooding and poor water quality, especially around the Ciénaga de la Virgen, a large lagoon in the city, and the Canal Juan Angola that links it to Cartagena Bay.

Water as Leverage is a Colombian–Dutch cooperation to improve the city's water system and the living conditions of all its residents, and to make Cartagena more climate resilient and inclusive.

The approach

The consortium worked in phases: conceptual designs first, then three designs at pre-feasibility level, and finally one design taken to feasibility, including whether it can be financed.

The solutions are circular and nature-based: bringing back transport over water, reusing dredged material, restoring mangroves to protect the coast, and reusing plastic waste to keep drainage channels open while creating local jobs.

The challenge

A good design only matters if someone will pay to build it. The total programme is too large for any single financier, so the question was which projects to start with, and with whose money.

What we did

  • Mapped the international financial stakeholders, from multilateral and bilateral lenders to NGOs and funds, with their goals and rules.
  • Gave an overview of the international funding and financing sources open to the programme.
  • Recommended starting with visible, bankable first projects and growing the pipeline step by step, with the city closely involved.
  • At the start of phase 2, reassessed the finance work for the two priority projects and the sources of money available to the city.
  • Took part, with Arcadis and Witteveen+Bos, in the joint talks with financial institutions.
