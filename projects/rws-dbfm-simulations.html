DBFM simulations for Rijkswaterstaat

Learning to manage a 25-year DBFM contract in one day, by playing both sides of the table.

Client
Rijkswaterstaat
With
Madenco
Period
2022
Our role
Simulation content and game design

The project

Rijkswaterstaat trains its people in DBFM contracts with network simulations in which participants play the client and the private consortium. There are two variants: DROOG, based on a road, and NAT, based on a waterway lock.

The challenge

After two years without sessions, both simulations had to be brought up to date before Rijkswaterstaat could offer them again.

What we did

  • Updated, together with Madenco, the game documents and objects of both simulations.
  • Ran through and tested the game engine, restored links to changed documents and updated the calculation cases.
