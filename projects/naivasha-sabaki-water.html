Naivasha and Sabaki, Kenya

Naivasha and Sabaki bulk water

Two water PPPs in preparation in Kenya, and an independent view for their financier on fees, agreements and value for money.

Location
Naivasha and Sabaki, Kenya
Developers
Royal HaskoningDHV and Rebel, for the Government of Kenya
We worked for
Invest International, with Arcadis
Period
2025
Our role
Financial and contractual review

The project

Kenya is preparing bulk water supply as public-private partnerships. The Naivasha project brings drinking water and wastewater services to the special economic zone and the town; Sabaki is a second bulk water project.

A consortium of Royal HaskoningDHV and Rebel develops the projects on behalf of the Government of Kenya. Invest International supports the development phase.

The challenge

In a developer-led PPP, the financier pays for development before the project exists. Invest International wanted to know whether the proposed fees and budgets were reasonable, and whether the joint development agreement protected its position.

What we did

  • Assessed the risks in the term sheet of the joint development agreement.
  • Analysed the proposed development fee and success fee against international practice.
  • Checked the price and value for money of the development budgets for Naivasha and Sabaki, component by component.
  • Prepared the questions Invest International needed answered before committing, on fees, currency risk and payment triggers.
