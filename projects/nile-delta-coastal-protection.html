Nile Delta coast, Egypt

Coastal protection of the Nile Delta

A feasibility study for protecting the Nile Delta coast with nature-based solutions, and for the partnership that would deliver them.

Location
Nile Delta coast, Egypt
Client
Invest International
Lead consultant
Arcadis
Period
2026 to 2027
Our role
Procurement plan, PPP and contract management

The project

The low-lying coast of the Nile Delta is under pressure from erosion and a rising sea. This feasibility study looks at protecting it with nature-based solutions: working with natural processes rather than only against them.

Invest International commissioned the study. Arcadis leads it; P3nomads is part of the team as sub-consultant.

The challenge

Nature-based protection is only feasible if it can also be procured, contracted and maintained for decades. The study therefore has to answer who builds it, who looks after it, and on what terms, alongside the technical questions.

What we did

  • Lead the procurement plan: which contracts the project needs and how to tender them.
  • Lead the PPP approach: whether and how public and private parties can share the work and the risks.
  • Advise on contract management for the long life of the measures.
