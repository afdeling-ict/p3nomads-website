Hebron, Palestinian Territories

Maintenance of a specialised hospital in Hebron

Keeping a new hospital's medical equipment in working order, through a well-prepared maintenance tender.

Location
Hebron, Palestinian Territories
Employer
Palestine Red Crescent Society
We worked for
Invest International, with Arcadis
Period
2025
Our role
Review of bidding documents

The project

The PRCS Specialized Hospital in Hebron was built and equipped with support from the Dutch ORIO programme. With construction finished and all equipment installed, the hospital has entered its operations and maintenance phase.

The challenge

The maintenance of the medical equipment had to be tendered. The bidding document needed to be sound before Invest International could issue its no objection and the tender could be published.

What we did

  • Reviewed the bidding document for the maintenance of medical equipment.
  • Delivered a review memo to Invest International as the basis for its statement of no objection.
