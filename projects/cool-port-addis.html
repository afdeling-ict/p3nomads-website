Modjo Dry Port, Ethiopia

Cool Port Addis

A national cold-chain hub for fresh produce, linked by rail to the port of Djibouti, and procured step by step under the eye of its financiers.

Location
Modjo Dry Port, east of Addis Ababa
Owner and tendering authority
Ethiopian Maritime Authority
Financed by
The Ethiopian government and Invest International
We work for
Invest International, with Arcadis
Period
2025 to present

The project

Cool Port Addis is a cold storage and logistics facility for perishable goods at Modjo Dry Port, connected to the Addis–Djibouti railway and expressway. It gives Ethiopian growers a cooled route to export markets.

The first phase is financed by the Ethiopian government and by Invest International, through a grant from the Dutch DRIVE programme. For its first years the facility is to be run under a management contract with an international cool logistics expert.

The challenge

Building and operating the facility takes several contracts, each tendered by the Ethiopian Maritime Authority. Every procurement has to meet both Ethiopian public procurement rules and the requirements of the grant, and the financier needs to be sure that each step is sound before the next one starts.

What we did

  • Reviewed the procurement plan for the project's contracts.
  • Review tender documents and evaluation reports independently on behalf of Invest International, including the selection of the international cool logistics expert.
  • Propose concrete text changes rather than general remarks, so documents improve in fewer rounds.
  • Issue a statement of no objection once a document meets the standard.
