Ziguinchor, Senegal

Port of Ziguinchor

Renovating the port of Ziguinchor to revive trade in the Casamance region, starting with a sound contract for surveying the river.

Location
Ziguinchor, Casamance, Senegal
Contracting authority
Agence Nationale des Affaires Maritimes
We worked for
Invest International, with Arcadis
Period
2024
Our role
Contract review

The project

Invest International intends to contribute to the renovation and development of the port of Ziguinchor, to improve trade and living standards in the Casamance region.

One of the first steps is a contract for bathymetric surveys of the Casamance river.

The challenge

Before co-financing the survey contract, Invest International wanted to know which risks needed attention and how to resolve them.

What we did

  • Carried out a high-level review of the draft contract for the bathymetric surveys.
  • Followed up with an additional review and concrete suggestions to resolve the issues we found.
  • Discussed the findings and next steps with Invest International.
