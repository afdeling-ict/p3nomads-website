Quality in public procurement

Sharing Dutch practice on buying for quality, not only for price.

Location
Bratislava, Slovakia
Period
2021
Our role
Knowledge sessions on procurement

The project

In 2021 we shared Dutch experience with public procurement in Bratislava: how to give quality a real place in a tender, and how to procure energy-efficient buildings.

What we did

  • Presented how to balance price and quality with MEAT, the most economically advantageous tender.
  • Showed how to keep the focus on quality throughout the procurement process.
  • Explained ways to procure energy-efficient buildings, from best-value tenders to availability contracts with energy service companies.
