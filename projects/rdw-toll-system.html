The Netherlands

Toll system on the Blankenburg Connection and ViA15

Fitting a new toll system onto motorways that are built and maintained under DBFM contracts.

Client
RDW
Roads
Blankenburg Connection and ViA15
Period
2021 to 2022
Our role
Interface requirements

The project

Tolls on the Blankenburg Connection and the ViA15 are collected through number plate recognition, carried out by the RDW. The equipment for this has to be installed beside and above motorways that private DBFM contractors build and maintain for Rijkswaterstaat.

The challenge

Three parties work on the same asset: the RDW, its own contractor and Rijkswaterstaat's DBFM contractor. The RDW's contract had to secure the interests of all three, and prevent conflicts between the contractors.

What we did

  • Drafted the requirements for coordination with the DBFM contractor during design, construction and maintenance.
  • Set the technical interface requirements for mounting and connecting the toll equipment.
  • Recorded the information the RDW's contractor needs, including the relevant DBFM requirements and performance regime.
  • Prepared the requirements for agreement with Rijkswaterstaat.
