San Vicente Bay, Chile

Dredging at the San Vicente terminal

Enlarging the berthing capacity of an oil terminal in a busy bay, with the contractor's knowledge brought in early.

Location
San Vicente Bay, Chile
Client
ENAP, Empresa Nacional del Petróleo
Period
2023
Our role
Advice on early contractor involvement

The project

ENAP wants to enlarge the berthing capacity of its terminal in San Vicente Bay, which plays a key role in supplying crude oil and products to and from the refinery. That requires dredging around the existing jetty.

The challenge

The bay is congested and used by local fishers, the environmental stakes are high, and the jetty is busy on both sides. To get approval, ENAP needed reliable information on dredging methods and their impact. Only contractors can give that, and involving them early must not compromise a fair tender later.

What we did

  • Assessed the room for pre-competitive interaction with dredging contractors within ENAP's procurement rules.
  • Designed a tender and contract strategy for early contractor involvement.
  • Set up a knowledge base for early contractor involvement and contract management, including probity and procurement assurance.
  • Advised on procuring the project management office that will steer the contractor.
