Soc Trang City, Mekong Delta, Vietnam

Sustainable water supply in Soc Trang

Climate-resilient drinking water for a city facing salt intrusion, and an investment case to pay for it.

Location
Soc Trang City, Mekong Delta, Vietnam
Water company
SOCTRANGWACO
Programme
WaterWorX Mekong Delta water operators' partnership
With
VEI
Period
2021

The project

Soc Trang lies close to the sea and already suffers severe salt intrusion in both surface water and groundwater. Many customers receive water that does not meet Vietnamese and WHO standards.

The local water company, SOCTRANGWACO, works with VEI and other partners in the WaterWorX programme, funded largely by the Dutch Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

The challenge

Resilient water supply needs investment that the water company cannot fund alone. One of the programme's aims is better access to investment finance, through proposals for domestic and international financiers.

What we did

  • Prepared a PPP scoping study and an investment teaser for interested investors.
  • Described the investment options and their financial and institutional set-up, with specific attention to PPP finance.
  • Laid the basis for terms of reference to study the feasibility of six options.
